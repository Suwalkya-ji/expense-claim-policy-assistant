# Acme Corp Employee Expense Policy (v2026.1)

## 1. General Principles
<!-- id: GEN-1 -->
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable, necessary expenses incurred while conducting company business. Expenses must be business-related, documented, and submitted honestly. Personal expenses are never reimbursable, even when incurred during a business trip.

## 2. Submission Requirements
<!-- id: SUB-1 -->
Every claim must include the claimant name, expense date, category, amount, currency, and a description that states the business purpose. Claims missing any of these fields will be returned for clarification.

## 2.1 Submission Window
<!-- id: SUB-2 -->
Claims must be submitted within 90 days of the expense date. Claims older than 90 days require approval from Finance and must be routed for manual review. Expenses dated in the future cannot be claimed.

## 2.2 Duplicate Claims
<!-- id: SUB-3 -->
Each expense may be claimed once. Submitting the same expense more than once (same claimant, date, amount and currency) is prohibited. Suspected duplicates are held for review and must not be approved until the reviewer confirms they are distinct expenses.

## 3. Receipts
<!-- id: REC-1 -->
An itemised receipt is required for any single expense of USD 25 or more (or the equivalent in another currency). Expenses under USD 25 may be claimed without a receipt if the description states the business purpose. If a receipt is lost, the claimant must provide a written explanation; a reviewer may approve at their discretion.

## 4. Meals
<!-- id: MEAL-1 -->
Meals while travelling on business, or meals with clients and prospects, are reimbursable. The limit is USD 75 per person per day for individual travel meals. Client entertainment meals are limited to USD 150 per attendee and the description must list the attendees and the business purpose. Alcohol is reimbursable only as part of client entertainment and must not exceed 30% of the bill. Meals with only fellow employees, outside travel, are not reimbursable unless pre-approved as a team event.

## 5. Travel – Airfare and Rail
<!-- id: TRAV-1 -->
Economy class airfare and standard-class rail are reimbursable for business travel. Business-class airfare is permitted only for flights longer than 6 hours and requires prior approval. The per-claim limit for airfare is USD 1,500. Seat upgrades, loyalty program fees, and in-flight entertainment are not reimbursable.

## 5.1 Travel – Ground Transport
<!-- id: TRAV-2 -->
Taxis, ride-share (e.g. Uber, Lyft), public transit, airport shuttles, parking, and tolls incurred for business purposes are reimbursable up to USD 100 per claim. Car rental is reimbursable for compact or mid-size vehicles; fuel for rental cars is reimbursable. Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at USD 0.67 per mile. Commuting between home and the regular office is not reimbursable.

## 6. Lodging
<!-- id: LODG-1 -->
Hotel accommodation is reimbursable up to USD 250 per night. Claims above the nightly limit must state the number of nights in the description. Room service, minibar, laundry for stays under 5 nights, and in-room movies are personal expenses and are not reimbursable.

## 7. Office Supplies and Equipment
<!-- id: OFF-1 -->
Office supplies and small equipment (keyboards, mice, headsets, cables, stationery) needed for work are reimbursable up to USD 200 per claim. Items above this amount, including laptops, monitors and phones, must be procured through IT and are not reimbursable through expense claims.

## 8. Software and Subscriptions
<!-- id: SOFT-1 -->
Software licences, SaaS subscriptions and online services required for the employee's role are reimbursable up to USD 100 per claim, provided the tool is not already offered through company IT. Personal streaming, gaming, or entertainment subscriptions are not reimbursable.

## 9. Training and Professional Development
<!-- id: TRN-1 -->
Courses, certifications, conference registrations and professional books relevant to the employee's role are reimbursable up to USD 1,000 per claim. The description must name the course or event.

## 10. Client Gifts
<!-- id: GIFT-1 -->
Gifts to clients or partners are reimbursable up to USD 50 per recipient. The description must name the recipient organisation. Cash or cash-equivalent gifts (gift cards) are prohibited.

## 11. Telecommunications
<!-- id: TEL-1 -->
Business mobile phone and internet charges are reimbursable up to USD 80 per month. International roaming charges incurred on business trips are reimbursable with a receipt.

## 12. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
<!-- id: NONR-1 -->
The following are never reimbursable: personal entertainment, fines and penalties (including parking and speeding tickets), personal grooming, clothing, childcare, pet care, political or charitable contributions, and expenses already reimbursed by a client or third party.

## 13. Foreign Currency
<!-- id: FX-1 -->
Claims may be submitted in USD, EUR, GBP, INR, CAD or AUD. Amounts are converted to USD at the company's configured rates for limit checks. Other currencies must be converted by the claimant and are returned for clarification.

## 14. Review and Approval
<!-- id: REV-1 -->
All claims are reviewed by a human reviewer. Automated checks and AI-assisted analysis are advisory only. Reviewers may approve, reject, or request clarification, and may override the expense category with a documented reason. Every decision is recorded in the claim's audit history.
